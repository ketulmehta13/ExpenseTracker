# Saving money with the app

## Finding where your money goes
Start by seeing where your money goes: ask the assistant "where did my money go this month?", open Reports for spending by category, or use the period buttons on the Transactions page to compare this month with last month. The biggest category you don't need is usually the best place to cut.

## Subscriptions and unusual payments
The Insights cards list your recurring payments and subscriptions, and flag payments that are unusually large for that merchant or category. Cancelling subscriptions you've stopped using is one of the quickest savings.

## Setting a budget in the app
Set a monthly budget in Settings. The dashboard then shows how much of it you've used and the forecast card estimates where your spending will end this month, so you can slow down before you go over. For budgeting methods such as 50/30/20, ask the assistant.

## What the assistant can't do
The assistant works only with your own data in this app and general money knowledge. It doesn't recommend specific investments, give tax or legal advice, check the weather or the news, or chat about other topics.
