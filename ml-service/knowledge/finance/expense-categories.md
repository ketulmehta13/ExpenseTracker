# Expense categories and fixed vs variable costs

## Why categorise expenses
Categories turn a long list of payments into a few numbers you can act on: how much went to food, rent, travel or shopping. They show which part of your spending changed when your total goes up, and they are the basis of category budgets.

## Fixed and variable expenses
Fixed expenses stay about the same every month: rent, EMIs, insurance premiums, school fees and subscriptions. Variable expenses change with your choices: groceries, eating out, fuel, shopping and entertainment. Fixed costs are hard to cut quickly, so most short-term savings come from variable categories.

## Needs and wants
Needs are expenses you must pay to live and work, such as rent, basic groceries, utilities and commuting. Wants are the rest, such as dining out, new gadgets and holidays. The same category can contain both: groceries are a need, but a lot of premium snacks are a want.

## Keeping categories useful
Use a small number of categories that match how you spend, and file the same merchant the same way every time. Too many categories make it hard to see patterns; too few hide them.
