# Adding, editing and deleting transactions

## Adding a transaction
Choose Income or Expense at the top of the form, then fill in the title (for example "Groceries"), the amount and the date. The date defaults to today in your own time zone. A category is optional, and you can tick "Mark as recurring transaction" for regular payments such as rent.

## Category suggestions
While you type a title, the app suggests a category, for example "Suggested: Food · 94% sure — tap to apply". It first looks at how you filed similar titles before ("from your history") and otherwise uses a model trained to recognise Indian merchants. Suggestions appear only when the app is confident, and nothing changes until you tap the suggestion.

## Editing a transaction
Open Transactions and use the pencil icon on a row to change any field. On phones, the edit and delete buttons are always visible on each card.

## Deleting a transaction
Use the bin icon and confirm. Deleting is permanent, so your totals and charts update straight away.

## Searching and filtering
The Transactions page lists 25 transactions per page. You can search by title, filter by type, category, date range and amount range, and sort by date, title or amount.
