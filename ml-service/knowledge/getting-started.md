# Getting started

## Creating an account
Sign up with your email address, a username and a password of at least 8 characters. If email confirmation is on, we send you a link: open it to activate your account, then sign in. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

## The dashboard
After signing in you land on the dashboard. It shows your income, expenses and net balance for the selected period (this month, the last 30 days, the last 3 months or all time), each compared with the previous month. Below them are an income-versus-expenses chart, a spending-by-category chart and your most recent transactions.

## Adding your first transaction
Use the Add button (in the bottom bar on phones) to record an expense or income. Give it a title, an amount and a date; a category is optional. You can also type a whole transaction in one line with Quick Add, such as "swiggy 450 yesterday".

## Moving around
The menu has Dashboard, Transactions, Reports, Categories and Settings. Your profile menu in the top corner has your plan, your settings and sign out.
