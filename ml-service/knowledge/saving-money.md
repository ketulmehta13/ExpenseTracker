# Saving money with the app

## Tips to spend less and save more
Start by seeing where your money goes: ask the assistant "where did my money go this month?" or open Reports. Cut the biggest category you don't need, cancel subscriptions you've stopped using, and pay yourself first by moving savings out on payday.

## A simple rule of thumb: 50/30/20
A common starting point is to keep needs (rent, bills, groceries) to about 50% of income, wants (eating out, shopping, entertainment) to about 30%, and save about 20%. Adjust it to your situation.

## An emergency fund
Many people aim to keep three to six months of essential expenses aside for emergencies before saving for other goals.

## What the assistant can't do
The assistant works only with your own data in this app. It doesn't give investment, tax or legal advice, check the weather or the news, or chat about other topics.
