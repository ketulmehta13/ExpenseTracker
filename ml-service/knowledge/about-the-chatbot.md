# About the chatbot

## Who made the chatbot and what is its name
This chatbot is the Expense Tracker assistant. It was built by Ketul Mehta as part of the Expense Tracker app. It is an AI assistant, not a person.

## What the chatbot can do
The chatbot explains money topics such as budgeting, saving, debt and investing basics, and helps with using the app, showing the sources it used. It also answers questions about your own money: how much you spent or earned in a period or category, a summary of a period, where your money went, your biggest and latest payments, your average spending, how this month compares with last month, and how much of your budget is left. You can ask in everyday words, including Hindi-English.

## What the chatbot can't do
The chatbot can't add or change transactions or your budget: use the Add Transaction form, Quick Add, Scan bill or Settings for that. It doesn't recommend specific investments, or give tax or legal advice. For money topics the articles don't cover, it gives a general answer and labels it as general information. It doesn't answer questions unrelated to money or the app, such as the weather or the news.

## Your privacy in the chatbot
Your question, your recent messages in that conversation and the help passages that match go to Groq, the AI service that chooses how to answer and writes the explanations. Your transactions are not sent to it: answers about your own money are calculated by the app. You can rate answers with a thumbs up or down, and each rating is linked to that answer. Your saved conversations are private to your account, you can delete them, and deleting your account removes them.
