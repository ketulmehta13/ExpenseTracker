# Categories

## Default categories
Every account starts with eight default categories: Entertainment, Food, Other, Rent, Salary, Shopping, Travel and Utilities. They are marked "Default" and can't be renamed or deleted, but you can use them as you like.

## Creating your own
Open Categories and choose New category to add your own: type a name and pick a colour. The icon is chosen from the name (for example a cart for "Groceries"). You can create as many custom categories as you like.

## Deleting a category
When you delete a category that has transactions, you choose another category to move them to first, so no transaction is lost or left uncategorised by accident.

## Categories and suggestions
The category suggestions in the transaction form use your own category names. If you file Amazon under "Household", the app learns that from your history and suggests "Household" next time.
