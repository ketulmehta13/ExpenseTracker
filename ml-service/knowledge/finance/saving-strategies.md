# Saving strategies

## Set a savings rate
Decide what share of your income you will save, for example 20%, and treat it like a bill. A rate is easier to keep than a fixed amount when your income changes.

## Automate it
Schedule transfers to a savings account, recurring deposit or SIP for the day after your salary arrives. Automatic saving removes the monthly decision, and money you don't see in your spending account is spent less.

## Cut the biggest leaks first
Look at your largest spending categories over the last three months rather than small daily costs. Food delivery, shopping and unused subscriptions are common leaks. Cancelling one ₹499 monthly subscription saves about ₹6,000 a year.

## Give savings a goal
Savings for a named goal (an emergency fund, a trip, a down payment) with a target amount and date are easier to stick to. Divide the target by the number of months left to get the monthly amount.

## Watch lifestyle creep
When income rises, spending tends to rise with it. Saving at least half of every raise keeps your savings rate growing instead of staying flat.
