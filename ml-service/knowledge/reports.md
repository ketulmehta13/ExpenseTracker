# Reports

## What the Reports page shows
Reports turns your transactions into charts: daily income and expenses, spending by category, and your totals for the period. Use it to see where your money goes over time.

## How much history you can see
Reports and the transaction list cover your full history, including older transactions. If older transactions seem missing, check the date range you've selected.

## Totals that look wrong
Check the selected date range first: the dashboard compares the chosen period with the previous month. Transactions dated in the future are counted on their date, and income and expenses are always totalled separately.
