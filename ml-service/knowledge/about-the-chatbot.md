# About the chatbot

## Who made the chatbot and what is its name
This chatbot is the Expense Tracker assistant. It was built by Ketul Mehta as part of the Expense Tracker app. It is an AI assistant, not a person.

## What the chatbot can do
The chatbot explains money topics such as budgeting, saving, debt and investing basics, and helps with using the app, showing the sources it used. It also answers questions about your own money: how much you spent or earned in a period or category, where your money went, how this month compares with last month, and how much of your budget is left.

## What the chatbot can't do
The chatbot can't add or change transactions or your budget: use the Add Transaction form, Quick Add, Scan bill or Settings for that. It doesn't recommend specific investments, or give tax or legal advice. It answers only from the app's help articles and your own data, so it says "I don't know" about anything else, such as the weather or the news.

## Your privacy in the chatbot
Your question and the help passages that match it go to Groq, the AI service that writes the answers. Your transactions are not sent to it: answers about your own money are calculated by the app. Your saved conversations are private to your account, you can delete them, and deleting your account removes them.
