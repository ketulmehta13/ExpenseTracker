# Monthly budget and month-end forecast

## Setting a budget
Open Settings and enter your monthly budget, then save. The budget is the total you plan to spend in a calendar month, across all categories. Set it to 0 to turn it off.

## The month-end forecast
The forecast card on the dashboard estimates where your spending will end this month. It adds up what you've spent so far, your usual everyday spending for each remaining day of the week, and bills or subscriptions still due this month. It shows a likely range as well as a single number.

## On track, at risk or over budget
With a budget set, the card says "On track" when the forecast is within your budget, "At risk" when the forecast is above it, and "Over budget" once you have already spent more than the budget. It also shows the chance of going over, such as "very unlikely to go over".

## Why the forecast changes during the month
Early in the month the range is wide because most of the month is still ahead. It narrows as days pass, and an unusually large one-off payment is not treated as everyday spending.
