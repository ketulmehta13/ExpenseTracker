# Quick Add: type a transaction in one line

## How it works
Type a transaction in plain words in the Quick Add bar on the dashboard, such as "swiggy 450 yesterday", "₹2,499 amazon headphones" or "salary credited 92k on 1st", and press Enter. The transaction form opens already filled in with the title, amount, date, type and a suggested category. Nothing is saved until you check it and tap Save.

## What it understands
- Amounts: 450, ₹450, rs 450, 450/-, 1,25,000, 1.5k, 2 lakh.
- Dates: today, yesterday, kal, aaj, parso, 2 days ago, last friday, 3rd oct, oct 3, 12/10 (day first).
- Income: words such as received, salary, credited, refund or cashback make it income; "maid salary" stays an expense.

## When an AI model helps
If the app can't read part of your line (for example "ola 250 3 days back"), an AI model may fill the gap. Its amount must appear in your text, and the app tells you which fields it filled so you can check them before saving.

## Tips
Put the amount as a number. If a date is ambiguous, write it as "3rd oct" or "12/10".
