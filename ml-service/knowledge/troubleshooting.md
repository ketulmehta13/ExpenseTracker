# Troubleshooting

## I didn't get the confirmation or reset email
Wait a few minutes and check your spam or promotions folder. Make sure you typed the right email address, then request the email again.

## No category suggestion appears
Suggestions start after you type at least three characters and only appear when the app is confident. They aren't shown while editing an existing transaction or after you pick a category yourself.

## I can't create another category
On the Free plan you can create up to 5 custom categories. Delete one you no longer use or upgrade to Pro for unlimited categories.

## The export buttons are locked
CSV and PDF exports are part of Pro. On the Free plan the buttons show a lock and take you to the plans page.

## I was signed out
The app signs you out after 15 minutes without activity. Sign in again; nothing you saved is lost.

## Insights are empty
Insights need history: about four weeks of transactions for the forecast, three payments for a subscription and eight payments at a merchant or category before anything can look unusual.
