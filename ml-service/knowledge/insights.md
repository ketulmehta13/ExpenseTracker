# Spending insights

## Unusual spending
A payment is marked unusual when it is at least 6 times your usual top payment at the same merchant, such as ₹3,141 at a cab service where you normally pay up to ₹285. If you've paid that merchant fewer than 8 times, it is compared with the rest of its category instead. Payments under ₹500 are never flagged, and regular bills are left out. Unusual payments from the last 60 days appear on the dashboard and get an "Unusual spending" badge on the Transactions page.

## Subscriptions and bills
The app spots payments that repeat on a rhythm (weekly, every 28 days like a prepaid mobile plan, monthly, quarterly or yearly) with a steady amount, such as rent, Netflix or a gym membership. The card lists them with how often they come, the amount and the next expected date, plus their total per month. Frequent but irregular spending, like ordering food a few times a week, is not counted.

## The monthly summary
Above the cards, a few sentences sum up your month: what you've spent so far compared with the same point last month, where the month is heading, your biggest category and anything unusual. All numbers are calculated by the app. When an AI model writes the wording it is labelled, and the app checks it never adds numbers of its own.

## When insights appear
Insights need some history: the forecast needs about four weeks of transactions, a subscription needs at least three payments, and unusual spending needs at least eight payments to compare with.
