# Privacy and the AI features

## What the AI features use
Category suggestions, insights and Quick Add run on the app's own service. They read your transactions with your own sign-in, under the same protection as the rest of the app, and nothing is stored separately. The models are small and run on our server; your data isn't sold or used to advertise to you.

## AI language models
Some wording, such as the monthly summary or help with a hard-to-read Quick Add line, may come from an AI language model. It receives only what it needs: the line you typed, or monthly totals and category names, never your full transaction list. Every number it shows is calculated by the app.

## Turning suggestions off
Suggestions never change anything by themselves: a category suggestion is only applied when you tap it, and Quick Add only fills in a form for you to check. If you don't want them, simply ignore them.

## Currency
The currency setting changes the symbol used to display amounts and is remembered on each device. It doesn't convert amounts between currencies.
