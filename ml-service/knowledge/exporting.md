# Exporting your data

## CSV and PDF export
On the Transactions page, use Export CSV or Export PDF to download your transactions with the current filters applied. Exports are a Pro feature; on the Free plan the buttons show a lock.

## Opening the CSV in Excel
The CSV is saved so that Excel shows the ₹ sign and Indian-language text correctly. Values that start with = + - or @ are made safe so a spreadsheet can't treat them as formulas.

## The PDF
The PDF is a printable table of your transactions with totals. Amounts are written with "INR" because the standard PDF fonts can't print the ₹ sign.

## Downloading everything before deleting your account
Export a CSV from the Transactions page first: deleting an account removes all of its data permanently.
